How the score works (manual-trading aid — reads the book, never trades)

Every setup — ACTIVE, PROBE and SHADOW — stamps once per 5-min scan while its entry conditions hold. A signal drops off ~7.5 min after its trigger window closes (conditions fail, session ends, or entry cutoff).

Each firing signal contributes status_weight × evidence pips toward its side. status_weight: ACTIVE 1.0 · PROBE 0.6 · SHADOW 0.25 — then discounted ×0.6 per governor strike 🔻 (a struck record keeps costing until redemption). evidence = 0.5·broker truth + 0.3·era avg + 0.2·7-day form, renormalized over available parts. Broker truth = the governor's 21-day trust score of REAL completed cycles (decayed mean R × 60p, shrunk n/(n+4)) — actual banked fills outrank any simulator record; 🏦 marks broker-backed rows. Sim parts shrunk x·n/(n+8) — unproven setups say ~nothing. Contributions are then scaled by path shape: ×(1 + 0.5·tilt·sign(evidence)) with tilt = (MFE−MAE)/(MFE+MAE) from era-median excursions — own-side pushes strengthen on MFE-heavy paths, CONTRA pushes strengthen on MAE-heavy paths (the MAE-flip rule); neutral under 5 episodes. Card tgt/heat = contribution-weighted typical favorable / adverse excursion of the aligned signals (swapped for contra rows). A firing setup with negative evidence pushes the opposite direction (MAE-flip doctrine) and is tagged CONTRA.

confidence = 100·tanh(|net|/8)·(0.5+0.5·agreement) where agreement=|net|/gross. Distance = contribution-weighted expected pips of net-aligned signals. Est. time in trade = contribution-weighted median realized hold from the shadow-sim episode store (exit_bar×5m, censored excluded; horizon fallback). Evidence stores refresh every 15 min; this page every 30 s.

Track record: every 5 min a cron snapshots each pair's consensus; the first snapshot of a run is a CALL, graded on forward executable price (ask-entry long / bid-entry short, opposite-side exits) at +30m/+1h/+2h/+4h/+8h. The strip above the cards shows hit-rate and avg pips overall and per confidence band; each card's hist shows this pair+direction's own record. Samples reset when the formula changes.
