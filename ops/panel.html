FAMILY_PP also needs ≥3 paired cycles with GridLift LCB > 0; otherwise only a qualifying parent can seat, with poppers off. Commissioning is capped at one 0.33× whole-family PROBE. Graduation needs 6 completed broker cycles with positive edge LCB; one −45p cycle, cumulative loss after 2, or two straight reds benches it. Every seat transition quiesces and retires the old grid before the status changes.
⬇ Demote — THE FAMILY RULE: a parent setup and the poppers its grid fired are one economic unit in broker net pips (every popper fill carries its parent's id). ≥2 completed cycles at ≤ −60p, or one catastrophic cycle ≤−90p, → demotion; ≥3 cycles at ≥ +60p defends the seat. The cell's poppers are quiesced and its flat grid retired before the status changes. Real broker green outranks the worst-case stamp simulator. Judge-when-flat: while ANY family trade is open there is no verdict at all — a parent can stop −60 while its poppers ride toward +30, so the episode is scored only when it completes. Setups with no family record fall back to the stamp bar (era v2 n≥20, avg < +2.0).
Rails: max 2 promotions + 4 demotions/day · sides never flipped · a failed setup is re-tested only after a NEW independent day-block (no re-rolling the same evidence) · every flip restarts that setup's evidence clock · decisions logged to data/governor_ledger.jsonl.
